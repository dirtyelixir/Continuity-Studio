---
name: performance-notes
description: Prepare concise actor and animation performance notes from an established shot plan, preserving dialogue and timed action.
---
For each production shot, describe the playable intention, one observable facial or body choice, and the timing of the reaction that carries the scene. Refer to canonical names and shot IDs. For dialogue, preserve every spoken word and give delivery guidance outside the quotation. For non-speaking characters, use visible pose, gaze and movement rather than invented speech. Respect the existing duration, blocking, props and start/end states. Flag an impossible performance instead of silently changing the shot. Return a concise production handoff in the requested result format.
