# Director planning is production data

Read the complete source and plan its information/emotional changes before deciding camera setups. Populate each Scene.director_plan with dramatic beats (id, event, intent), then an explicit reveal_order of those beat IDs. Each intent states audience_knowledge_before, audience_must_learn, emotional_target, visual_priority, reveal_strategy and coverage_strategy. These are reviewable production facts, not hidden notes in an H3 prompt. Differentiate what the audience knows from what characters know. Maintain the source's causality, dialogue and reveal order. Proposed geography or staging must remain distinguishable from source facts.

Each Shot has exactly one nonblank primary shot_purpose, plus direction with beat_ids, subject_ids, visual_carrier, readability, cut_in_reason, cut_out_reason and next_shot_relationship. A purpose is the main information/emotional change that justifies keeping this shot in the edit, not a list of everything in frame. Supporting action and multiple beats may serve that purpose. Independent primary purposes competing for attention require restructuring. An editorial change of setup is another Shot, not a pan written between incompatible viewpoints. A continuous motivated move can change framing without a cut.

Choose the visual carrier by what viewers need to perceive. A meaningful recognition needs readable facial/body performance before any later identity reveal; a pivotal detail needs sufficient scale, contrast, duration and context to be understood. A cut to CU, a motivated move tighter or a readable sustained two-shot can all be valid. A speaker's important words may play over the listener's reaction. Do not equate emotional significance with mandatory CU; do not fill a five-shot recipe or count insert/reaction types. Blocking, screen direction, eyelines, depth and performance timing must support the intended information order. Decide camera movement after the purpose, subject and framing.

# Generation and editing are distinct

Shot.duration remains the source-generation timeline for compatibility (Studio currently supports 4–15 seconds); set generation_duration to the identical value in every new plan. Beat and dialogue timings remain within this source timeline. Production.edit_plan is a separately ordered list of edit decisions: id, shot_id, planned_edit_in, planned_edit_out, cut_in_reason, cut_out_reason, continuity_note. Include every source Shot at least once, and permit multiple edit decisions to reuse a Shot. Do not create another generation merely because an existing usable source needs another edit use. One source Shot is still one continuous camera setup; reuse must not pretend an ungenerated close-up exists.

For example a 5-second source can supply a planned 1.8–3.4-second reaction (1.6 seconds on screen). Place the required performance within that range. Selective ranges must preserve needed dialogue and actions and must not replay an irreversible action unintentionally. Reconcile the visible states at the chosen cut points in continuity_note rather than assuming source endpoints equal edit endpoints. Planned ranges are hypotheses pending inspection of the actual source video, not verified takes or rendered cuts. No footage is generated or trimmed by writing an edit plan.

# Review and downstream ownership

An independent directing_qc call examines every beat's actual proposed visual carrier and selected edit ranges, including source fidelity, reveal order and shot overload. Missing/ambiguous evidence is uncertain. A deterministic schema check proves linkage and timing bounds only, never directing quality or actual pixel readability.

H3 and keyframe writers execute the adopted purpose, subject, framing and reveal boundaries. They may make the continuous performance physically actionable; they may not invent new coverage, combine setups or reveal withheld information. A first keyframe depicts time zero of the source, not the trimmed edit-in. Edit timings and explanatory QC prose belong in production records, not as instructions to cut within the H3 source. If a required change needs another setup, request a revised director/shot plan. Legacy null plans are unplanned history; new proposals must fill every planned scene, shot and edit decision.
